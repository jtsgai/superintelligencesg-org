## What this connector does
The SI Commons connector lets Meta's Muse agent search the public Superintelligence SG directory of Singapore AI organisations, programmes and ecosystem statistics. It is read-only. It cannot create, change or delete anything, and it makes no purchases.

## Data we receive
- **Your request:** the search words and options Muse sends on your behalf, for example "AI governance organisations".
- **Technical data:** IP address, time, and the tool used, which your device or Meta's servers send with each request.
We do not ask for, and do not receive, your name, email, Muse or Meta account details, contacts, location, payment data or any login.

## How we use it
Only to return the results you asked for, and to keep the service secure and working (rate limiting, abuse prevention, troubleshooting). We do not sell data. We do not use it for advertising or profiling, and we do not use it to train AI models.

## Retention and deletion
We do not store the text of your searches. Our service code does not log or store IP addresses. Cloudflare, as the hosting provider, may process IP addresses in its own request logs under its own policy (https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/). To ask about or request deletion of any data, email info@jt-sgai.com. We reply within 30 days.

## Sharing
- **Cloudflare, Inc.:** our hosting provider, which processes requests on our behalf.
- **Meta:** receives the results it requested so it can show them to you.
- **Others:** we share data with others only where the law requires it.

## Security
- All traffic uses HTTPS.
- Access keys are stored encrypted, and only authorised personnel can access our systems.
- If an incident affects user data, we will notify Meta within 48 hours and act as required under Singapore's Personal Data Protection Act 2012 (PDPA).

## Directory content
Directory records describe organisations from public sources, and each record is listed with its source link. Superintelligence SG is founded by Jerry Tuo (Jingtao Tuo), who also founded JT M&C and co-founded Red Fun Planet. Listings affiliated with our founder get no special ranking.
