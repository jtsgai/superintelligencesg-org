Subscribe to SG AI Weekly

One issue a week covering Singapore AI funding, new projects, events, policy and one organization of the week. Every item links to its original source and publication date; items without a source are left out.

Enter your email below. A confirmation email will be sent; your subscription starts only after you click the link in it. You can also subscribe on the SG AI Weekly page on Buttondown.

Sign up

After you submit, check your inbox for a confirmation email from SG AI Weekly. You can also follow the Signals updates on the Navigator.

In each issue

  • Funding: publicly announced rounds by Singapore AI companies.
  • New projects: new products, programmes and platforms.
  • Events: AI events held or announced in Singapore.
  • Policy: AI policies, programmes and guidance from government agencies.
  • Organization of the week: one profile from the ecosystem map.
