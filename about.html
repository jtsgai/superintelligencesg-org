A public place to publish.

The Commons is an open directory for organizations that want to describe their work in their own words and make it easier to find.

Open by default.
Clear about status.

Joining is voluntary and immediate. An organization can share its logo, name, founder and core work without waiting for approval.

The profile is visible as soon as it is submitted. The directory shows whether the entry is self-published, claimed or reviewed.

That status describes the source of the information. It is not a ranking or an endorsement.

What a profile means.

Submitted

The organization published the profile directly. It is public and clearly marked as self-published.

Claimed

The organization confirmed that it represents the profile. Claiming confirms representation, not quality.

Reviewed

We checked basic public facts such as the name, location or homepage. Review does not recommend the organization.
